# Healthcare AI Strategy: A Conditional Investment Case

**Independent hypothetical educational case study | Work in progress**

How should a mid-sized hospital decide whether to invest in an AI-supported clinical deterioration alert system? This portfolio case combines a physician's clinical perspective with structured business analysis, requirements traceability and governance. It examines the entire pathway from observation and prediction to human assessment, escalation and evaluation.

## Executive view

The original assessment recommends **no hospital-wide deployment on the current financial case**. A capped, controlled 12-week evaluation on two internal medicine wards is a conditional option only if clinical, technical, governance and funding prerequisites are met. A pilot would test feasibility and safety processes; it would not itself establish clinical benefit or authorize scale-up.

The baseline's hypothetical three-year model yields **−44.6% ROI**: €120,000 initial cost, €90,000 recurring annual cost and €72,000 annual gross benefit. The later **−12% scenario is illustrative and unreconciled**: its assumptions and ROI bridge have not been established. Neither figure is an observed pilot result. The proposed 35% shorter assessment time and 15 additional minutes of alert work per shift are hypothetical scenarios, not measured outcomes.

## Method and contribution

The author is a physician developing a portfolio for healthcare, digital health and clinical AI strategy consulting. Her case exercises include clinical workflow and safety reasoning, stakeholder engagement proposals, requirement prioritization and acceptance criteria. The baseline also preserves her opportunity ratings. AI assistance supported critical discussion, synthesis, English editing and the organization and drafting of these repository materials; proposed additions are labeled as such. This repository is a structured consolidation, not a verbatim transcript or a claim that all analysis was independently completed. The author should review the proposed wording and ownership before publication.

Evidence status matters: the baseline is an earlier version; subsequent user-authored exercises are identified in the documents; working tables include AI-assisted proposals; and planned frameworks are not completed analyses. No real hospital deployment, patient data, empirical results, clinical vendor selection or compliance determination is claimed.

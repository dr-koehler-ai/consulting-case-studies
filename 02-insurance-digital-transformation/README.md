# SecureShield Insurance | Digital Transformation Strategy

**Strategic Assessment · Business Problem Solving · Digital Transformation**

## Project Overview

This case study was developed as part of the **IBM Management Consultant Professional Certificate**.

The project examines a fictional regional property and casualty insurer experiencing a market share decline from 12% to 9% over two years amid increasing competition from digital-first InsurTech companies.

The objective was to structure the business problem, explore potential root causes, and develop a preliminary strategic consulting approach.

## Approach & Deliverables

- **Problem Structuring:** Problem classification and 5 Whys root-cause analysis.
- **Consulting Engagement:** Proposed 10-week diagnostic and strategy development plan.
- **Stakeholder & Ethics Assessment:** Key stakeholder concerns and professional ethical considerations.
- **Strategic Options:** Evaluation framework for building, partnering, or acquiring digital capabilities.
- **Executive Communication:** Five-slide presentation outline for senior leadership.

## Key Learnings

This project strengthened my ability to distinguish symptoms from root causes, develop testable business hypotheses, structure consulting engagements, and differentiate strategic alternatives from implementation initiatives.
---

*Educational case study based on a fictional business scenario. Analyses and recommendations are preliminary and do not represent a real client engagement.*
